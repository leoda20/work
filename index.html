About me

I’m a Product Designer who likes turning complexity into simple, well-defined solutions. Over the years, I’ve learned that good design isn’t just about the final interface — it’s also about consistency, documentation, accessibility, business context, and getting the team aligned around a shared direction.

I’m naturally drawn to patterns and systems, which helps me spot opportunities and connect the dots between user needs and business goals.

Lately, I’ve also been experimenting with AI as a collaborator and accelerator — mostly by building small tools that adapt to the way I work and make me more productive, without compromising design standards or critical thinking.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me experimenting with coffee ☕️, cooking 🍳, planning my next trip ✈️, or drawing ✍️.

Thomson Reuters

User Communication

Thomson Reuters had accumulated dozens of fragmented emails and notifications across different teams, creating inconsistent experiences and unnecessary user noise. I helped redesign the communication strategy by auditing existing messages, consolidating content, and creating a scalable email design system.

Role:

UX Researcher

Tags:

Messaging

UX

Research

Understanding the problem

Mapping the communication ecosystem

Before redesigning the experience, it was necessary to understand the scale of the problem. I collaborated with product teams across the platform to audit every existing email and notification, mapping each message to its purpose, audience, and stage in the user journey.

Scroll to view all
Output
  • 87 unique messages identified
  • Multiple overlapping communications discovered
  • No shared communication standards between teams

defining context

Identifying what users actually needed

Using the Eisenhower Matrix, each communication was evaluated based on urgency and value. This process was made with all the team stakeholders and helped teams identify redundant notifications, merge similar messages, and prioritize communications that genuinely supported user workflows.

Visual and content

Establishing consistency

While working on the eisenhower matrix, it was possibler to notice that each team had developed its own email style and tone, without shared patterns or guidelines, communications felt disconnected from the Thomson Reuters product experience.

Reducing noise through content strategy

Based on the findings, we eliminated unnecessary messages, combined overlapping notifications, and rewrote key communications using UX writing principles.

Before

  • Multiple emails for a single workflow
  • Inconsistent tone and structure
  • Inconsistent visuals

After

  • Fewer, clearer messages
  • Improved information hierarchy
  • More actionable communication

Creating a scalable email design system

To prevent future inconsistencies, a modular design system was created with reusable email templates in Figma based on common communication scenarios. These templates gave teams a shared foundation while allowing designers to create new emails faster.

Highlight:

  • Modular layouts
  • Consistent branding
  • Faster future production

Outcome

Less noise, clearer communication

With all the changes, it was possible to reduce redundant emails and standardize communication across Thomson Reuters, clearing content and generating reusable templates to reduce interruptions and make experiences more consistent across teams.

Reducing communication overload

Reduced notifications, resulting fewer emails per month across companies.

Creating a scalable system

Built reusable templates that improved consistency and simplified future email creation.

Aligning teams around users

Unified teams around communication priorities by focusing on message value, urgency, and user needs.

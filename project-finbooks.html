Finbooks

Web Designer

UI Designer

Project

Finbooks is an AI-powered financial tool designed to help users create campaigns more efficiently. The redesign focused on making AI the core of the experience while simplifying the interface and improving user confidence.

Goals

  • Bring campaign settings, options, and controls into a single streamlined experience.
  • Make credit availability and usage visible throughout the workflow.
  • Position AI as the central value proposition instead of just another feature.

Design Approach

The redesign introduced a cleaner visual hierarchy, reducing distractions and creating more space for the AI functionality to stand out.

Content was reorganized around the user's main goals, helping users understand the product value before interacting with it.

Results

🌀

Simplified Sections

Throughout the interface, superfluous text was minimized to ensure a more streamlined user journey. Layout improvements were meticulously approached, adhering to a pixel-perfect design methodology for enhanced aesthetics and readability.

⏺️

Minimalistic Design Philosophy

To create a cleaner, more contemporary feel, the design underwent a significant shift towards minimalism. This entailed streamlining content, simplifying sections, and adopting a recent design language.

✨

AI-focused positioning

The interface was redesigned around the AI experience, making its capabilities immediately visible and helping users understand the product's main benefit.

🎯

Improved conversion experience

The hero section was redesigned with a stronger message hierarchy and clearer CTA placement, creating a more focused path toward user action.

💬

Clearer messaging

A strategic change was made in the main heading from "ending paycheck-to-paycheck cycle" to "escape the paycheck trap," aimed at succinctly capturing the essence of the AI tool's benefits.

📟

Reduced friction

A complex contact form was replaced with a simpler email capture interaction, making it faster for users to engage.

Outcome

The redesign created a more focused and modern AI experience by:

  • Making AI capabilities immediately understandable
  • Reducing interface complexity
  • Improving content hierarchy and readability
  • Creating a stronger conversion-focused landing experience

Next Steps

Future iterations would focus on validating the redesign through user behavior analysis, conversion metrics, and feedback loops to continuously improve the experience.

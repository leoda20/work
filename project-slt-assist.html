Energyline AI Assist

An AI-powered writing assistant that helps users improve text directly inside the platform. By selecting text within an input, users can generate and refine suggestions without leaving their workflow.

Overview

Writing often involves more than getting words onto a page. Users need to adjust tone, improve clarity, and find better ways to communicate.

SLT Assist explores how AI can support these tasks directly within the writing experience, removing the need to switch between tools or copy content elsewhere.

The goal was to make AI feel like a natural part of the product, rather than a separate destination.

The challenge

AI should support the workflow, not interrupt it.

Traditional AI writing tools often require users to leave their current task, paste content into another interface, and manually bring the result back.

This creates unnecessary steps and breaks focus.

The challenge: How might we make AI assistance contextual, intuitive, and easy to control?

Designing the interaction

From text selection to better writing.

I designed the experience around a simple principle: AI should appear when users need it, not compete for attention when they don't.

The interaction follows four steps:

01 — Select

Users highlight the text they want to improve. This keeps the interaction focused on a specific part of their content.

02 — Rewrite

A contextual Rewrite action appears near the selection, making AI assistance available without opening another tool.

03 — Review

AI generates an alternative and presents it alongside the original text, allowing users to compare both before making a decision.

04 — Refine

Users can regenerate suggestions based on how they want the text improved, keeping control over the final wording.

Designing the motion

Motion as functional feedback

Motion wasn't added for decoration. It helps users understand how their actions connect to the AI response.

I focused on five moments:

  • Rewrite action appearing after text selection.
  • Suggestions entering the interface.
  • Feedback during AI generation.
  • Transitions between alternative suggestions.
  • Regeneration as part of the same interaction.

These transitions make the experience feel connected and provide feedback without interrupting the writing flow.

The final experience

AI that feels part of the product

The final concept connects text selection, rewriting, generation, and refinement into one continuous interaction.

Users can improve specific content, compare suggestions, and decide what to keep — all without leaving the original input.

The result is a more contextual approach to AI assistance, where technology supports the user's intent rather than taking over the task.

Outcome

Less switching. More control.

SLT Assist demonstrates how AI can improve an existing workflow through contextual actions, clear feedback, and purposeful motion.

By keeping suggestions close to the original content, the concept reduces unnecessary steps while allowing users to remain in control of their writing.

What I learned

Designing AI interactions requires more than generating useful outputs. Users need to understand what AI is doing, review its suggestions, and decide what happens next.

Keeping AI contextual and giving users control makes the experience feel more predictable, transparent, and useful.

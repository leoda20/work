Project

Designing a white-label NFT marketplace that helps restaurants increase customer loyalty through exclusive digital memberships.

Challenge

Create a marketplace that makes blockchain benefits understandable to everyday customers while allowing restaurants to launch branded NFT loyalty programs.

Research insights

We interviewed 20 participants to understand dining habits, blockchain awareness, and willingness to adopt NFT-based loyalty programs.

60%

Visit bars or restaurants 2–4 times per month, making recurring rewards attractive.

50%

Had little or no understanding of blockchain or NFTs.

90%

Would purchase an NFT if it unlocked meaningful discounts and exclusive experiences.

Personas

Business Model Canvas

We mapped the business model to validate the value proposition, customer segments, and revenue opportunities before designing the product.

Sitemap

We designed two journeys tailored to each audience.

Customers

Buy and manage NFT memberships.

Business owners

Learn about the platform and launch branded marketplaces.

Objective

Educate restaurant owners about the platform while reducing uncertainty around blockchain and NFT adoption.

  • Simplified blockchain language
  • FAQ addressing common concerns
  • Clear conversion path to request a marketplace

Visual identity

The visual identity combines technology with community through gradients, modern typography, and a flexible brand system that adapts to different restaurant identities.

📲

Incorporating primary colors that resonate with technology.

🔤

Opting for a sans-serif font to maintain a consistent tone.

Key Takeaways

20 user interviews

Validated user behavior before defining the solution.

White-label architecture

Enabled multiple restaurant brands to launch consistent marketplaces with minimal customization.

Simplified Web3 onboarding

Reduced blockchain complexity by introducing NFTs through familiar loyalty concepts instead of technical terminology.

Final Considerations

Designing for blockchain required translating a complex technology into familiar user experiences. The project reinforced the importance of reducing cognitive load before introducing new concepts, proving that adoption depends more on clarity than on technology itself.
